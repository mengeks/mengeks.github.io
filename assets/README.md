Xiang Meng is a Post-Doc at Dana-Faber Cancer Institute, working with [Hajime Uno](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ROrcYf0AAAAJ&hl=en). He completed his PhD candidate in the Department of Statistics at Harvard University, with Natesh Pillai (Chair), Luke Miratrix and Iavor Bojinov on causal inference.
Prior to Harvard, Xiang earned his Master of Science in statistics from the [University of Washington](https://stat.uw.edu/), worked with Prof. [Thomas Richardson](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/tsr/website/inquiry/home.php) on [coherent modelling of causal effects](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/biom.13687?casa_token=sf87P2ANuFsAAAAA:F3CLiuOaec35WR-65s_Mm65raXrEw6mMAd1rGEuCkHz5LAdtnJvZBsa9wPguhR2EQzSScs6RRL1lJg). He completed an internship with the Clinical Modeling & Evidence Integration team at [Sanofi](https://www.sanofi.us/). He holds a Bachelor of Science in Quantitative Finance from National University of Singapore (2018).
