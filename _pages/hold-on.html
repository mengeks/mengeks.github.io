Hold On · 等一下

A calm little sand garden that keeps you company while your AI is working, and blooms a flower when the task is done.

  • Pops up softly over your screen on macOS when Claude Code or ChatGPT desktop starts a task.
  • Pour water, dig channels, plant seeds, and follow a duck around. No levels, no points, no countdown.
  • When the task finishes, a flower blooms and one click takes you back to your AI.
  • Runs entirely on your own Mac: nothing is sent anywhere. In English and 中文.
